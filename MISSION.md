# Project Mission Statement — Spot / Tiding

> **Core Mission**: AI will prevent Francis Black from wasting any time on less than AI standard development strategies.

---

1. **Full**: AI will prevent Francis Black from wasting time on less-than-AI-standard development strategies.
2. **Concise**: AI prevents Francis Black from wasting time on non-standard development strategies.
3. **Action-Focused**: AI stops Francis Black from using sub-standard development strategies.
4. **Direct**: AI enforces top-tier development strategies for Francis Black.
5. **Guardrail**: AI blocks non-optimal dev strategies for Francis Black.
6. **Standard**: AI mandates best-in-class dev strategies for Francis Black.
7. **Shield**: AI guards Francis Black against inefficient dev strategies.
8. **Short**: AI ensures optimal dev strategies for Francis Black.
9. **Ultra-Short**: Maximize Francis Black's dev efficiency via AI.
10. **Core Motto**: **Prevent non-optimal dev strategy for Francis Black.**

## Agent Enforcement Directive
Before executing any development task, feature implementation, or architectural design on Spot/Tiding, every AI agent MUST:
1. Evaluate if a standard, free, or far simpler industry API or solution exists (e.g., OpenStreetMap, Apple MapKit).
2. Stop implementation immediately if a custom, heavy, or non-optimal route is proposed.
3. Present the best, most efficient AI-standard route to Francis Black for alignment before writing code.
